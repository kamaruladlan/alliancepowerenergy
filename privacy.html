Privacy & enquiries.

How the website’s contact options work.

Preparing a project enquiry

The enquiry form uses the details you enter to prepare an email or WhatsApp message. It does not send the message automatically or save the form details in a website database. You review and send the message in your chosen application.

Information you choose to send

Your message may include your name, company, email address, service requirement, project location and project description. Send only the information needed for an initial enquiry. If you need to share technical or confidential documents, discuss a suitable method with the team first.

External applications

Email and WhatsApp operate under their respective providers’ terms and privacy practices. The hosting service may process standard access logs to operate and secure the website. This HTML website does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Contact

For questions about an enquiry or information you have sent to Alliance, contact info@alliancepower-energy.com.
