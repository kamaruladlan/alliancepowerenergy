About Alliance

Expertise, working together.

Alliance Power & Energy Group brings engineering services and specialist partners together across energy, marine, and infrastructure. We help shape a clear approach to complex project requirements, from the initial conversation to coordinated delivery.

A shared direction

Connecting the right people, disciplines and ideas.

Every project connects people, disciplines, and decisions. Alliance's approach centres on understanding the scope, identifying the right technical resources, and coordinating the interfaces between them. Our service areas span industrial and energy facilities, offshore and marine operations, and infrastructure equipment support. Through collaboration with specialist partners, we bring these capabilities into a focused discussion about your project's needs.
