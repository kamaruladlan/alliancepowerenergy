Scope & capabilities

Start with the requirement.

Marine operations call for the right vessel, equipment, and technical scope. Alliance's service offering includes discussion of lift boats, offshore support vessels, accommodation vessels, tugs and barges, dredgers, and LNG-related vessels. Each enquiry is assessed around the intended operation and project requirements; vessel availability, specifications, and commercial arrangements are established for the particular scope.
