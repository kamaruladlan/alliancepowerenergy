Useful to know

Before we begin.

What does EPC mean?

EPC combines engineering, procurement, and construction within a defined project scope.

Can I enquire about a solar project?

Yes. Share the location, intended application, approximate capacity if known, and current project stage.

What should I include in an enquiry?

A brief scope, available technical documents, target schedule, and the support you require.
